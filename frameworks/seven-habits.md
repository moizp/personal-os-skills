# Framework: The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People (Stephen Covey)

Generic summary for use by Skills. Do not put personal application notes
here — those belong in your Claude Project, separate from this file.

## Core ideas relevant to planning

- **Roles, not just goals.** A person operates in several life roles at once
  (e.g. professional, parent, health, community). Balanced planning sets
  goals *per role*, not just for whichever role is loudest that week.

- **Big Rocks first.** Time-block the important-but-not-urgent items
  (Quadrant II: important, not urgent) before the calendar fills with
  urgent-but-unimportant noise. If big rocks aren't scheduled first, they
  never get scheduled.

- **Begin with the end in mind.** Quarterly/annual goals should trace back
  to a small number of stated long-term outcomes or a personal mission,
  not be generated fresh each planning cycle.

- **Weekly, not daily, is the right planning unit.** Covey argues for
  weekly planning (aligned to roles and big rocks) with daily planning as
  lightweight execution of that week's plan — daily planning alone tends to
  be reactive.

## How this should show up in the system

- Quarterly goal-setting should ask: "which role does this serve?"
- Weekly review should check: "did the big rocks get scheduled, or did
  urgent noise crowd them out?"
- Daily planning should pull from the week's plan, not invent priorities
  from scratch each morning.
